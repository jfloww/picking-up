# English corrections — 2026-08-07

1. You wrote: "sometimes it too long to show."
   Better: "sometimes it's too long to show."
   Why: missing the verb "is" (or its contraction "'s") — "it too long" needs a linking verb between the subject and the adjective: "it is/it's too long."

2. You wrote: "currently, If I click it, then it changes to editible mode"
   Better: "Currently, if I click it, it switches to edit mode."
   Why: only capitalize the first word of the sentence ("Currently"), not "If" mid-sentence. "editible" → "editable" (spelling). "changes to X mode" reads more naturally as "switches to X mode."

3. You wrote: "I hope the tab comes from the botton in the taskdetails drawer"
   Better: "I'd like this panel to slide up from the bottom of the Task Details drawer."
   Why: "botton" → "bottom" (typo). "taskdetails" → "Task Details" (two capitalized words, matching the UI label). "comes from the bottom" is vague — "slides up from the bottom" describes the motion you mean more precisely. Also "tab" → "panel," since what you're describing isn't a tab (a side-by-side switchable view) but a stacked/nested panel.

4. You wrote: "please keep correction my phrasing"
   Better: "please keep correcting my phrasing."
   Why: "correction" is a noun; you need the verb form "correcting" after "keep."
